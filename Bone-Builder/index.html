Step 1 — Decide Which Option

We need to establish exactly what is changing between the current and new product.

⚠ TBD = still needs client confirmation
Product ElementCurrentNewStatus

Once the comparison is complete, determine:

Can the new Bone Builder remain on the existing ASIN?

Preferred: Use Existing ASIN

Retaining the existing ASIN is strongly preferred due to:

  • Existing Subscribe & Save subscriber base
  • Recurring revenue
  • Reviews and ratings
  • Sales history
  • Organic ranking and indexing
  • Existing PPC history

The final product specifications will determine which execution option we follow.

Option A — Existing ASIN Retained
1
Inventory and SKU Transition
  • Create a new SKU for the pouch under the existing ASIN
  • Separate old vs. new inventory by SKU
  • Establish old inventory sell-through timeline
  • Establish new inventory availability date
  • Plan controlled old SKU → new SKU switchover
2
Subscribe & Save Transition
  • Submit request to transfer subscribers from old SKU → new SKU
  • Coordinate subscriber transfer with inventory switchover
3
Develop Communication Asset Pack for Transition

Create dedicated assets explaining the 'Old Packaging → New Packaging' change before and during the transition:

  • Listing image
  • A+ transition module
  • Transition/explainer video
  • Brand Story asset
  • Store transition asset
  • Insert in remaining old inventory announcing the upcoming change
  • Dedicated Creator Connections transition campaign

Core Message: Same Bone Builder / Same Formula / New Packaging / Updated Serving

4
Post Transition Asset Update

Separately update all permanent Amazon content to accurately represent the pouch:

  • Listing images
  • A+ Content
  • Brand Story
  • Store
  • Cross-sell assets
  • Videos
  • PPC/custom advertising creatives where needed

Complete an audit of all Amazon assets to identify anywhere the old tub or outdated serving information remains.

5
Go Live
  • Confirm new inventory is received and ready
  • Execute old SKU → new SKU transition
  • Execute S&S subscriber transfer
  • Switch listing assets to new packaging
  • Update remaining brand assets
  • Maintain transition communication during initial switchover period
  • Monitor PPC/CVR during transition
6
Post-Transition Monitoring
  • Subscribe & Save subscriber count
  • Sales
  • Conversion rate
  • Voice of the Customer
  • Reviews
  • Returns/refunds

Once the new format is established and customer confusion risk is low, phase out the temporary transition messaging.

Option B — New ASIN Required
Strategy

NEW PRODUCT LAUNCH + EXISTING CUSTOMER MIGRATION + SUBSCRIBER REBUILDING

1
New ASIN Launch
  • Create standalone new ASIN
  • Create full New Product Launch Asset Pack (SEO, copy, image stack, A+)
  • Integrate into Brand Story
  • Integrate prominently into Store
  • Establish launch offer
  • Launch Vine
  • Launch Subscribe & Save when available
2
Old ASIN → New ASIN Transition Pack

Use the existing Bone Builder presence to educate current customers about the replacement:

  • Listing image to communicate the transition
  • A+ transition module
  • Transition/explainer video
  • Brand Story transition messaging
  • Store transition messaging
  • Insert in remaining old inventory
  • Consider using 'Newer version' widget

Core Message: Same Bone Builder / Same Formula / New Packaging / Updated Serving

3
Traffic and Search Visibility
  • Launch PPC for the new ASIN
  • Capture existing Bone Builder / Healthy Bones branded demand
  • Gradually shift PPC investment from old → new ASIN
  • Launch dedicated Creator Connections campaign
  • Prioritize new ASIN within the Store
  • Gradually establish new ASIN as the primary Bone Builder destination
4
Subscriber Recovery

Because existing subscriptions cannot be assumed to carry over to a new ASIN:

  • Begin customer education before old inventory disappears
  • Clearly communicate that the formula remains the same
  • Promote Sub&Save enrollment on the new ASIN
  • Align Creator Connections campaign with Sub&Save recovery
  • Track new subscriber acquisition against the existing subscriber baseline
5
Post-Launch Monitoring
  • New ASIN sales & CVR
  • PPC performance
  • New Subscribe & Save enrollments
  • Subscriber recovery vs. old ASIN baseline
  • Old vs. new ASIN sales split
  • Old inventory sell-through
  • Voice of the Customer / customer feedback
