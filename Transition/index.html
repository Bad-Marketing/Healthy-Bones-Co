Transition Strategy Overview

Nubonta will launch on Amazon in January 2027 as a new brand with new ASINs, while Healthy Bones remains live during the transition.

We will build Nubonta from the ground up and use the existing Healthy Bones presence to intentionally migrate customers, demand, and recurring purchasing into the new brand.

Healthy Bones =

Retain + Migrate

Nubonta =

Acquire + Grow

The goal is to protect revenue and customer trust while building Nubonta's reviews, rank, subscriber base, and sales history — then retire Healthy Bones product by product as each Nubonta replacement becomes established.

Healthy Bones Shifts to Retention + Migration

From Nubonta launch, Healthy Bones shifts away from active customer acquisition and becomes primarily a retention + migration channel.

  • Serve existing customers and subscribers
  • Protect existing revenue during the transition
  • Capture existing HBC/branded demand where valuable
  • Educate customers on the transition
  • Explain product changes
  • Show the relevant Nubonta replacement/options
  • Cross-link directly into Nubonta through A+
  • Direct future purchasing toward Nubonta

Stop active strategies designed primarily to acquire new-to-brand HBC customers or new HBC subscribers. This does not mean immediately switching off all HBC advertising. Profitable HBC demand capture can continue while products remain live, but we should no longer invest in building HBC as the future growth brand.

Nubonta Becomes the Growth Brand
  • Non-branded customer acquisition
  • New-to-brand growth
  • New subscriber acquisition
  • Creator Connections
  • Broader catalog growth
  • Incremental advertising investment

From launch, new customer and subscriber acquisition should be focused on Nubonta rather than Healthy Bones, so we are building future growth under the new brand.

Phase 5 — Retire Product by Product

Target: March/April 2027 · Extension Window: Through June 2027 Where Needed

Healthy Bones should not be retired on one universal date. Each product should exit once its Nubonta replacement is established enough to absorb demand.

  • Subscriber exposure remains high
  • Nubonta conversion/reviews are still weak
  • Organic visibility needs more time
  • HBC inventory remains
  • Customer migration is slower than expected

The two HBC Collagen products should also be evaluated separately. Use the Migration Tracker to guide individual retirement decisions while continuing to monitor the health of the combined business. June 2027 should be treated as an extension window, not the planned retirement date.

Advertising

There are two paths on the table:

  • Track A - Rebrand existing ASINs. Update the current listings to Nubonta. Reviews, subscribers, rank, and advertising history stay with the ASIN.
  • Track B - Launch Nubonta as a new brand with new ASINs. Build fresh, sell in parallel, migrate customers over, retire HBC product by product.

The critical constraint: Track A is not available for every product. Amazon only permits an in place brand change where the product is materially unchanged; same format, same serving size, same formula. Anything that changes size, servings, or combines products requires a new ASIN regardless of which track we choose.

Track A: Rebrand Existing ASINs

Advertising principle: the account keeps its history. This is a swap, not a relaunch.

What We Keep

  • Campaign performance history and conversion data on every rebranded ASIN
  • Keyword rank and relevance signals
  • Subscriber base (SKU swap within the same ASIN is permitted)
  • Review count and CVR
  • Amazon's algorithmic understanding of the listing
1
Prepare (Now to Nov 2026)
  • Audit every campaign for ASIN/SKU references that need updating at switchover
  • Build Nubonta branded campaigns (and pause them); "nubonta collagen", "nubonta bone health". Zero search volume today, but they need to exist from day one to capture volume as it builds
  • Keep bidding on and defending HBC branded terms, this volume persists for a year or more and now points at a Nubonta branded listing. This is free warm traffic
  • Build DSP purchase and subscriber audiences (still valuable for transition messaging, even though customers aren't moving listings)
  • Capture advertising baseline by ASIN so we can detect any post-switch dip
2
Introduce (Dec 2026)
  • Run the founder transition video in Sponsored Brands Video on existing listings, no incremental spend, we already hold these placements
  • Light DSP awareness to purchaser and subscriber audiences
  • No structural changes. December is a high revenue month; we'll protect it.
3
Switch (Jan 2027)
  • Switch ads to the new SKU on each rebranded ASIN; because it's the same ASIN, this is straightforward and no advertising history is lost
  • Update SB and SD creative and headline copy to Nubonta branding
  • Enable the paused Nubonta branded campaigns
  • Maintain full HBC branded coverage, the listing is now Nubonta, so this traffic converts directly
  • Watch for a short CVR dip as customers encounter unfamiliar branding. Expect 1-3 weeks. Do not cut budgets reactively; hold spend and let the transition messaging work.
4
Stabilise (Feb-Apr 2027)
  • Monitor CVR and rank on rebranded ASINs against the Phase 1 baseline
  • Progressively expand Nubonta branded campaigns as that search volume grows
  • Sustain HBC branded capture, do not turn this off, it will deliver for a long time
  • Scale acquisition normally; no rebuild period to work through
  • Bone Builder and Daily Minerals follow their own launch ramp
5
Consolidate (Apr+)
  • Retire HBC-specific creative and copy once transition messaging has run its course
  • Fold HBC branded keyword capture permanently into Nubonta campaigns
  • Business as usual
Track B: New Brand, New ASINs

Advertising principle: we are launching a new brand and migrating demand into it. Everything rebuilds.

What We Lose

  • All campaign history and conversion data on new ASINs
  • Keyword rank - rebuilt from zero
  • Reviews and the CVR they support
  • Subscribers cannot transfer across ASINs; must be re-acquired
  • Amazon's algorithmic understanding
1
Prepare (Now to Nov 2026)

Audience build is the most time-sensitive task in the entire plan. A 365-day purchase audience built in January is nearly empty. Built in September, it's at full depth by launch.

AudiencePlatformPurpose
HBC 365-day purchasersDSPCore migration retargeting pool
HBC Sub&Save subscribers (~7,000)AMC → DSP/SDHighest value migration target
Product-level purchase audiencesDSPProduct specific migration creative
HBC detail page viewers, non purchasersDSP/SDWarm non converters to catch at launch

Known constraint: a prior broad "frequent purchasers not on Sub&Save" audience failed Amazon's minimum size threshold. The subscriber specific audience is narrower but better targeted, and subscriber count has grown substantially. Needs re-testing; failed queries can be revised and resubmitted.

Feasibility Questions to Close in This Phase

  • Can each audience be built at sufficient size?
  • Can DSP audiences built under HBC serve Nubonta creative?
  • Can Sponsored Display retarget HBC viewers with Nubonta ASINs?
  • Does AMC subscriber data survive the brand separation?

Blocking dependency: Nubonta Brand Registry gates Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display, and Store ads. Without it we launch on Sponsored Products alone, a serious handicap in month one. This needs a hard date.

Investment split: 100% HBC.

2
Introduce (Dec 2026)
  • Founder transition video into SB Video on HBC listings
  • Light DSP awareness to purchaser and subscriber audiences
  • No Nubonta campaigns, nothing is purchasable; any spend is wasted
  • Do not reduce HBC spend

Investment split: ~95% HBC / ~5% transition awareness.

3
Launch (Jan 2027)

The core mechanic: anyone searching "Healthy Bones" should see Nubonta.

Priority 1: Capture HBC branded search volume with Nubonta ads. This volume doesn't disappear in January. It's the warmest, highest-intent traffic available at a fraction of non-branded cost. SP Exact on "healthy bones collagen", "healthy bones co", "fortibone", "bone coach collagen" → pointing at Nubonta ASINs. Bid aggressively; it's our own brand equity.

Priority 2: Dual brand Sponsored Brands. Both brands in a single ad unit with transition headline copy. Does the migration explanation inside the ad and lends established credibility to the new brand.

Priority 3: Nubonta non branded acquisition. All new-to-brand investment moves to Nubonta. SP Exact on Tier A terms, SP Auto for discovery, Phrase for harvest. Expect elevated ACOS for 60-90 days; this is the cost of new ASINs and should be budgeted, not treated as underperformance.

Priority 4: DSP retargeting. Nubonta creative to the HBC purchaser and subscriber audiences from Phase 1. Highest leverage DSP spend of the transition.

On the HBC side: stop non branded acquisition, maintain branded defense (protects the subscriber funnel from competitor conquesting during our most exposed window, AlgaeCal is the named risk), continue profitable demand capture while inventory lasts, remove the 25% first subscription incentive.

Investment split: ~70% HBC / ~30% Nubonta.

4
Migrate (Jan to Apr 2027)
  • Monthly budget reallocation, gated on Nubonta CVR and review velocity rather than the calendar
  • Weekly, bi-weekly search term harvest; Nubonta is building keyword history from nothing and needs active mining
  • Amplify the strongest Creator Connections migration content into SB Video and SD
  • Sustain Nubonta bidding on HBC branded terms throughout
  • Cannibalisation audit monthly; HBC and Nubonta competing for the same impression wastes spend. Structure negatives so each owns distinct terms, except where dual brand SB is intentional.
5
Retire (Apr-Jun 2027)

Shift each ASIN's budget to its Nubonta successor once: CVR is within ~10% of the HBC product's historical CVR, review volume no longer suppresses conversion, organic rank is established on core keywords, and HBC inventory is drawing down.

Maintain residual HBC branded capture permanently under Nubonta campaigns, that traffic persists long after the products are gone.

Final state: 0% HBC / 100% Nubonta.
